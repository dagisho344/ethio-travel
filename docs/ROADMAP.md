# Roadmap

## Phase 0 Foundation
Monorepo, Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL/Prisma, Redis, config, Swagger, logging, CI.

## Phase 1 Marketplace MVP
Sprint 1 Auth/RBAC
Sprint 2 Regions/Cities/Destinations/Attractions
Sprint 3 Business onboarding/members/location/hours/media
Sprint 4 Verification/admin approval/audit
Sprint 5 Services + category extensions
Sprint 6 Search/filter/map/Near Me
Sprint 7 Favorites/reviews/dashboards

## Phase 2 Transactions
Sprint 8 Availability/booking
Sprint 9 Payments/refunds
Sprint 10 Messaging/notifications

## Phase 3 Planning
Sprint 11 Trip planner
Sprint 12 Recommendations/AI, analytics, subscriptions

## MVP Definition
Traveler discovers approved businesses/services by destination. Businesses register and submit complete profiles for verification. Admin controls publication. Search, filters, maps, favorites, reviews and core dashboards work on mobile/desktop.
