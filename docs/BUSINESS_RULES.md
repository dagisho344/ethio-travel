# Business Rules
BR-001 Only APPROVED businesses are public.
BR-002 Only authorized members modify a business.
BR-003 SUSPENDED businesses cannot accept new bookings.
BR-004 Verification decisions record reviewer/time/reason.
BR-005 Verification documents are private.
BR-006 A service can be discoverable but non-bookable.
BR-007 Money has explicit currency and decimal representation.
BR-008 Store lat/lng for geospatial entities where available.
BR-009 Booking and payment statuses are separate.
BR-010 Capacity is consumed transactionally.
BR-011 Frontend payment success is not authoritative.
BR-012 Verify provider webhooks.
BR-013 Preserve booking/payment/refund history.
BR-014 Reviews may require completed/verified interaction.
BR-015 Conversation access requires membership.
BR-016 Admin-sensitive actions are audited.
BR-017 REJECTED verification can be corrected/resubmitted.
BR-018 Validate media type/size.
BR-019 Private files use controlled/signed access.
BR-020 Cancellation/refund uses the policy captured for the transaction.
